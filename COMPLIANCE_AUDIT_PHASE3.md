## COMPLIANCE RULES FOR ALL CONTENT

### Rule 1: Verification Before Publishing
- **IF** you write a specific claim (number, name, achievement)
- **THEN** you must have documentation proving it
- **ELSE** remove the claim or mark it as "estimated/typical"

### Rule 2: Attribution & Sourcing
- **Major claims** need source citations
- **Case studies** need customer permission OR anonymization OR "example scenario" marker
- **Testimonials** need actual customer names/consent
- **Statistics** need source (World Bank, IEA, country statistics)

### Rule 3: Honest Uncertainty
- **IF** unsure about a number: "Approximately X" or "Typical range: X-Y"
- **IF** specific to one customer: "One customer achieved X"
- **IF** hypothetical: "Example scenario: if a facility had X, the ROI would be Y"

### Rule 4: Disclaimers Where Needed
- SLA claims → "Subject to contract terms and site conditions"
- ROI claims → "Your actual results depend on facility size, existing infrastructure, and utilization"
- Performance claims → "Verified across [number] customer installations"
- Market claims → "Based on [data source]"
