### 3️⃣ AFFILIATE & PARTNERSHIP PROGRAM (Week 2-3)
**Purpose:** Leverage partners to sell our solutions (recurring commission model)

**Target Partners:**
1. **Real Estate Agents & Developers**
   - Commission: 5% of project value (KES 500K-5M per property)
   - Pitch: "Add backup power = 30% premium pricing, faster lease-up"
   - Platform: Simple affiliate dashboard (form + tracking link)

2. **Electrical Contractors & Engineers**
   - Commission: 10-15% of installation
   - Pitch: "Recommend our generators to your clients, we handle everything"
   - Support: Technical training, sales materials, lead sharing

3. **Construction Companies**
   - Commission: 8% of temporary power contract value
   - Pitch: "Site generators are a pain. We handle rental, fuel, maintenance"
   - Platform: Project-based commissions (tied to site duration)

4. **Mining Consultants & Engineering Firms**
   - Commission: 5-10% of power system design project
   - Pitch: "Add backup power to every mining proposal, we execute"
   - Support: Co-branded proposals, technical review

**Affiliate Program Infrastructure:**
- Dashboard showing active referrals + earnings
- Marketing materials (one-pagers, email templates, social posts)
- Deal registration (prevent conflicts)
- Monthly commission statements
- Tiered benefits (higher earners get faster responses)

**Contract Elements:**
- Exclusive vs non-exclusive territories (for regional partners)
- Minimum performance targets
- Co-marketing requirements
- 12-month terms with renewals

**Estimated Leads:** 5-15 leads/week through partners (high-quality, pre-qualified)
