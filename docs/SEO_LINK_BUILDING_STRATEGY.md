# Link Authority Building Strategy — EmersonEIMS
**Goal:** Increase domain authority from 5/10 → 8.5/10 within 90 days through strategic link acquisition

---

## Current State
- Domain: ~6 months old (low inherent authority)
- Estimated backlinks: <50 (mostly self-created)
- Referring domains: <10
- Link anchor text: Generic ("click here", "emersoneims.com")
- Authority: Low but rising (recent case studies + schema help)

## Link Building Strategy — 3 Phases

### Phase 1: QUICK WINS (Weeks 1-2) — 15-20 links
**Target:** Easy, high-volume placements with moderate authority
**Method:** Directory submissions, business listings, local citations

#### 1.1 Business Directories & Listings (High Volume, Low Authority)
```
Target: 30+ listings → Domain mentions → Local SEO boost
Effort: 2-3 hours per site
Expected DA: 10-30
```

**Priority Targets:**
- Google Business Profile (PRIMARY — unlocks local search)
  - Local search visibility
  - Review ability
  - Local knowledge panel
  - Estimated authority lift: +1.5 DA

- Kenya Startup & SME Directories
  - KenyaStartups.com
  - Fuzu.com (SME platform)
  - Jumia Business (e-commerce directory)
  - Findacotechnician.com (generator/power specialists)

- Industry/Sector Directories
  - Kenya Chamber of Commerce & Industry (KCCI)
  - Construction Industry Council directories
  - Energy sector associations
  - Nairobi Business Assocation

#### 1.2 Local Citations (High Impact, Low Effort)

**Platforms:** Yellow pages, local directories, map listings

**Critical Big 3:**
- Google My Business (already done?)

- Bing Places

**Kenya-Specific:**
- SafariCom business directory

### Phase 2: AUTHORITY BUILDING (Weeks 3-6) — 30-50 links
**Target:** DA 30-50 sites (news, industry platforms, government)
**Method:** Press releases, partnership announcements, expert commentary

3. "AI-Powered Generator Selection Tool Launches in Kenya"

## Risk Mitigation

**What NOT to do:**
- ❌ Don't buy backlinks (Google penalty, instant death)
- ❌ Don't participate in link schemes/rings
- ❌ Don't overuse exact-match anchor text (looks unnatural)
- ❌ Don't spam directories or comments
- ❌ Don't submit to irrelevant low-quality sites

**Safe Practices:**
- ✓ Natural link profiles: 60% branded, 30% generic, 10% keyword-rich
- ✓ Diversified anchor text (brand name, URL, generic, keyword)
- ✓ Links from relevant contexts (power/energy sites link to power company)
- ✓ Gradual growth (avoid 50 links in one week = suspicious)
- ✓ Focus on authority, not volume (1 DA 50 link > 10 DA 10 links)
