## RISKS & MITIGATION

### Risk 1: Content Quality Consistency
**What Could Go Wrong:** Blog posts drop off, YouTube videos become infrequent, podcast guests cancel  
**Mitigation:** Fixed content calendar, dedicated content manager, guest booking 2 months in advance  
**Success Metric:** 95%+ publish consistency month 1-12

### Risk 2: Partner Quality Issues
**What Could Go Wrong:** Marketplace partner fails to deliver, money-back guarantee claims spike  
**Mitigation:** Rigorous 6-step audit before listing, trial projects required, quality oversight team  
**Success Metric:** <5% customer complaints, >95% partner retention

### Risk 3: Lead Conversion Rates
**What Could Go Wrong:** Leads from new channels don't convert as well as organic search leads  
**Mitigation:** Segmented nurture sequences, affiliate pre-qualification, targeted outreach  
**Success Metric:** 4-6% conversion rate (vs. 2-3% baseline)

### Risk 4: Competitive Response
**What Could Go Wrong:** Competitors copy our honest positioning, marketplace model, certification program  
**Mitigation:** Speed to scale (we're 6-12 months ahead), continuous innovation, brand loyalty  
**Success Metric:** Remain market leader despite competition

### Risk 5: Scaling Challenges
**What Could Go Wrong:** Can't hire quality personnel, lose key team members, systems break under load  
**Mitigation:** Early hiring (recruiting now for month 2 roles), documentation, redundancy in key roles  
**Success Metric:** Maintain service quality while growing 3-5x
